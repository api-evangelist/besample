Why Desktop-Optimized, Long Studies Won’t Take Us Beyond WEIRD (and What to Do Instead) Many behavioral studies are designed around a familiar participant profile: an undergrad sitting in a lab, at a desktop computer, focused, uninterrupted, and willing to spend a solid stretch of time on a task — anywhere from 25 to 60 minutes. This setup exists of course. But outside the lab — and especially outside the West — it’s not the norm.

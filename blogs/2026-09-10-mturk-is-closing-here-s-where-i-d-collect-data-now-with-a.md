On September 30, 2026, after more than 20 years, MTurk will permanently close. For researchers who have relied on it for participant recruitment — in psychology, behavioral science, economics, political science, computer science and beyond — that leaves a practical question: where do we collect our data now? The good news is that the answer is better than it would have been even ten or even five years ago.
